## Vocabulary Tokens

### 1. Type Tokens

Used to describe the production type of a part.

- `BB` — Broadband signal. Energy is spread across frequencies without clear harmonic bands. Appears as a smeared or noisy region on the spectrogram.
- `PULSED` — Pulsed signal where individual pulses or vertical lines are visible. Harmonic structure is not clearly tonal.
- `GAP` — Silence or absence of signal.
- `Tonal` — Default case. Do not include a token for tonal signals.

### 2. Length Tokens

- `SHORT` — Segment shorter than **0.2 seconds**.

(No long token for now.)

### 3. Contour Tokens

Used only for tonal harmonic signals (not for BB, PULSED, or GAP).

- `ASC` — Frequency contour clearly increases over time.
- `DESC` — Frequency contour clearly decreases over time.
- `FLAT` — Frequency contour remains approximately constant.
- `PEAK` — A clear local maximum (ascending followed by descending).
- `DIP` — A clear local minimum (descending followed by ascending).
- `SQUIGGLE` — Multiple successive ups and downs (series of peaks and dips). If the overall trend is rising or falling, combine with `ASC` or `DESC`.
- `UPSWEEP` — Short, rapid upward movement, typically at transitions (e.g., BB → tonal). Avoid using unless clearly distinct.
- `DOWNSWEEP` — Short, rapid downward movement with a curved or parabolic descent, often at the end of flat segments.

---

### 4. Speed Tokens

Used only with `ASC` or `DESC`.

- `FAST` — Rapid change in frequency.
- `SLOW` — Gradual change in frequency.

---

### 5. SBI Tokens (Side Band Interval)

Describe harmonic spacing in tonal signals.

- `SBI_TIGHT` — Harmonic bands are visually close together (approximately < 400 Hz spacing).
- `SBI_WIDE` — Harmonic bands are widely spaced (approximately > 1500 Hz spacing), often only 2–3 bands visible.
- `SBI_INC` — Harmonic spacing clearly increases over time.
- `SBI_DEC` — Harmonic spacing clearly decreases over time.

Use only when the effect is clearly visible and exaggerated.

---

### 6. Peak Modifiers

Used only together with `PEAK`.

- `LEFT` — Peak is asymmetric, leaning toward the left.
- `RIGHT` — Peak is asymmetric, leaning toward the right.
- `LARGE` — Peak spans a significant portion of the call.
- `HIGH` — Peak reaches an extreme frequency relative to the rest of the call.

Most peaks are small; only use modifiers when clearly justified.

---

### 7. Rare Tokens

- `BIPHO` — Biphonation. Harmonic stack present with a clearly overlapping second tonal component.
- `SINGLE_TONE` — Do not use for now.
